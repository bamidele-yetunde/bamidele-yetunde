### [Every health system cleared its elective backlog. The UK never did.](https://github.com/bamidele-yetunde/DataDNA_Health_System_Performance)
`Power BI` · `Power Query` · `DAX` · `Python (pandas, NumPy, matplotlib)`

[![Health system performance report](https://github.com/bamidele-yetunde/DataDNA_Health_System_Performance/raw/main/page1_executive_summary.png)](https://github.com/bamidele-yetunde/DataDNA_Health_System_Performance)

My entry for the **DataDNA November 2026 challenge**: 32 countries and **212 regional health systems** from 2018 to 2024, written up as a board report.

• Elective waits nearly doubled everywhere in 2021, but the UK was **still at 1.99× its 2019 wait in 2024** while no other country passed 1.67×  
• The UK spends **13.7% less per person** than EU members and has **1.66× the EU vacancy rate**  
• Staff burnout moves with vacancies (**r = 0.58**, or 0.73 within countries)  
• 6 page Power BI report on a star schema with **76 DAX measures**, plus a Python version that matches **all 22 headline figures**

### [Why 37% of hotel bookings never arrive, and what it's worth fixing](https://github.com/bamidele-yetunde/Hotel-Cancellations-Analysis)
`MySQL` · `Python (pandas, NumPy)` · `Power BI` · `Power Query` · `DAX` · `Excel`

[![Hotel cancellations dashboard](https://github.com/bamidele-yetunde/Hotel-Cancellations-Analysis/raw/main/page1_overview.png)](https://github.com/bamidele-yetunde/Hotel-Cancellations-Analysis)

End-to-end analysis of **119,390 bookings at two Portuguese hotels**, sized into three policy fixes worth **≈ €1.8M a year**.

- **37%** of bookings are cancelled or never show: **€16.7M** of booked stays lost in 26 months
- Portuguese group and offline travel-agent bookings are **22% of bookings but 45% of cancellations**
- **99.4%** of "Non Refund" bookings cancel, so the flag marks tentative blocks, not deposits actually paid
- 5-page Power BI dashboard on a star schema with **48 DAX measures** and an interactive overbooking what-if simulator

### [Why 97% of Olist customers never come back, and what actually drives 1-star reviews](https://github.com/bamidele-yetunde/olist-retention-delivery-analysis)
`MySQL` · `Python (pandas)` · `Power BI` · `DAX` · `Excel`

End-to-end analysis of **96,203 real e-commerce orders**, from raw data to a costed business recommendation.

- Late deliveries are **6.8% of orders but cause 37% of all 1-star reviews**
- **12 seller-to-customer routes produce 69% of late orders**; in 72% of them the seller shipped on time
- A 7-day buffer on the 12 worst routes would have rescued **40% of late orders at zero logistics cost**
- 5-page Power BI dashboard on a star schema with **52 DAX measures**; every metric defined once in a MySQL view and reproduced in pandas
